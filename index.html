The journey

Built for rigor. Now building with AI.

◆ Now building AI-powered apps

From 2017 to 2022, Fredrick worked as a licensed civil engineer — Project Engineer, Project Supervisor, and Design & Engineering Operations In-Charge across four companies — running site inspections, structural QA/QC, cost estimation, and cross-functional coordination on live construction sites. He passed the civil engineering licensure exam in November 2016.

In 2023 he rebuilt that foundation inside IT, applying the same inspect-before-you-trust discipline to enterprise systems: supporting network infrastructure at Accenture, then managing Tier-2 network and security operations for the Asian Development Bank project at Indra.

Today that discipline is pointed at a new frontier. He treats AI as a genuine collaborator in the build process — pairing with it on architecture decisions, data pipelines, and interface design — and is actively shipping two full applications this way: a hero-matchup analytics engine for Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, and a live cryptocurrency dashboard with its own ETL pipeline. The goal is a full move into AI engineering, building on an already-proven habit of verifying everything before it ships.
